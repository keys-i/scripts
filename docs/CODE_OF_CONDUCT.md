# Code of Conduct

## Our Promise

This repository is a personal collection of useful scripts that I also want to
be comfortable sharing with other people. Anyone who contributes should be
able to do so without harassment, intimidation, or needless hostility.

We welcome people regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual and romantic identity.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent while still taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information.
- Put the usefulness and safety of the scripts ahead of winning an argument.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, trolling, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious, deceptive, or undisclosed destructive
  behaviour.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional collaboration.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces used to
represent this repository.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/keys-i). Do not publish
personal or sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
kept as confidential as practical.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, and issues that do
not follow this code. Depending on the impact and pattern of behaviour,
responses may include a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent ban.
